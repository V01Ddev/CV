    resistor-transistor logic (RTL) board with AND, NOT, OR and NAND gates from
    discrete PN2222A transistors. Fully routed 2-layer board; first revision
    under debug.

    \item \textbf{RC Aircraft \& Radio Link}: Designed and built RC aircraft from
    first principles: airframe layout, carbon spar integration, servo geometry,
    and 3D-printed parts. Wrote 4-channel ESP-NOW firmware with a signal-loss
    failsafe.

    \item \textbf{Router Firmware Reverse Engineering}: Located the UART header
    on a Xiaomi router and captured boot logs over it, then extracted the
    firmware image from flash after finding the console locked.
